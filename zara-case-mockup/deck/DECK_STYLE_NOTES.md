## NGC house format (measured on the Ford Sp24 final deck)
- 16:9, 10 x 5.625 in.
- Type scale, stated on every slide: Title Raleway Bold 28; Subtitle Raleway Semibold 13; Kicker Lato 12; Body Lato Light 11; emphasis Lato Bold in the accent colour; sources and tracker Lato 10.
- Margins: content runs from 0.48 in to 9.51 in (about 0.5 in left and right). Header band from 0.22 in to 0.80 in; title starts at x 0.83 in; one-sentence takeaway line under it at y 0.80 in, x 0.67 in. Content area from about 1.28 in to 4.80 in. "Sources:" line bottom right at y 4.98 in. Section tracker and page number in a bottom band at y 5.30 in.
- Every content slide has: short topic title, a one-sentence takeaway directly under it with two or three bold phrases, two or three boxed modules with their own headers, a "Key Takeaway" box, a Sources line, a section tracker with the current section highlighted, a page number.
- Emphasis: key numbers and phrases bold and in the accent colour inside otherwise regular body text. Icons in circles as bullets. Thin 0.75 pt outlines on boxes, light fill.
- Deck skeleton: title, agenda + team, section dividers, content, Q&A, next steps (numbered 01 02 03), appendix with sources listed per slide.

## MBB conventions to apply on top
- Action titles: the title is the conclusion in a full sentence (max two lines), not a topic label. Reading only the titles should tell the whole story.
- One message per slide; the body proves the title. Pyramid principle: answer first, then three supports.
- Every chart has a so-what callout; every number has a source footnote; units and time period on every figure.
- MECE structure for the three issues / three solution pillars; consistent order of the three across all slides.
- Dense but aligned: strict grid, equal box widths, no decorative elements, generous use of white space around a small number of blocks.
- Typical exhibits: waterfall (per-garment economics), three-column scenario strip, 2x2 or table for case-study comparison, Gantt with decision gates, harvey balls for the pain-point table.

## Zara branding
- Black, white and one neutral grey only; replace NGC navy/blue with black, and the accent with black bold (magenta only if a single alert colour is needed, as in the app's SPECIAL PRICES).
- Typography: Zara's condensed serif logotype for the cover only; body in a clean grotesque (Helvetica Neue / Arial) with wide letter-spaced uppercase labels, as in the app; Bodoni Moda already used in the mock for the serif category switcher.
- Thin hairline rules, lots of white space, uppercase small labels, product photography full-bleed on the cover and dividers; phone screenshots from the mock as the visuals.
