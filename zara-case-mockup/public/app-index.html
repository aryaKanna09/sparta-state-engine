ZARA RELOOP

A pre-owned loop built inside the Zara iPhone app rather than bolted onto a separate website. Two flows — open either on a phone.

Buyer

Pre-owned stands beside new on every product, and buying it is the same flow as buying new.

  • Menu — pre-owned sits at 07, as it does today
  • Product page with SHOP PRE-OWNED beside ADD TO BAG
  • Sold-out size opens matches in your size
  • Listing, bag with the real $1 + 5% fee, confirmation
OPEN BUYER FLOW

Seller

The prompt is a price, not a demand claim. Photograph it, accept a grade, choose once how it travels.

  • Notification: sell this for up to $82, +$10 credit
  • Four coached photographs — the camera is real
  • Grade and price, with appeal
  • Drop-off or ship, then the sold alert and payout
OPEN SELLER FLOW
Running the seller demo The camera and its coaching are real — the three chips go black when exposure, sharpness and framing actually pass. Press 1, 2 or 3 to lock the grade before the last shot. If the camera is blocked, choose "Use the sample garment" and the flow still completes. The grade itself is simulated.
What is Zara's and what is ours Zara's, reconstructed: the app chrome, the numbered menu with pre-owned at 07, the four-tier condition ladder (New with tags / Like new / Good / Fair), MAKE OFFER beside ADD, the condition filter, market-price guidance, and the buyer-paid $1.00 + 5% fee. Ours: grading the condition instead of asking the seller to self-declare it, binding the price to that grade, routing the garment through a store so Zara can verify it, surfacing pre-owned on the new-product page, and the sold-out credit bonus.
