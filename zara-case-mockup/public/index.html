ZARA RELOOP

The size is gone. The garment is not. A shopper hits a sold-out size, and instead of a dead end Zara asks the people who already own that exact garment whether they want to sell it — grading and pricing it for them. Click straight through the flow below, or start at step 01 and use the bar at the bottom of every screen.
