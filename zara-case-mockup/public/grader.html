HOW TO RUN IT ON STAGEOpen this screen on a phone and hold a jacket up, or use the laptop webcam. The three chips go black when exposure, sharpness and framing pass — that part is genuinely measuring the frame. Press 1, 2 or 3 before the fourth shot to lock the tier to EXCELLENT, GOOD or FAIR so the result is predictable in front of judges. Space bar fires the shutter. If the camera is blocked, it falls back to a bundled photograph and still completes the flow.
WHAT IS REAL AND WHAT IS NOTReal: camera capture, and per-frame analysis of exposure, Laplacian sharpness and edge-framing, computed in the browser. Simulated: the condition grade itself. It is weighted by measured capture quality so it reacts to what you shoot, but it is not a trained condition model — say so if a judge asks, because the honest version of this answer is stronger than the vague one.
